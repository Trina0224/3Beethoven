# Coverage expansion teacher format and semantic review

The first 15 event questions were rejected under the original compact parser. A dated prompt amendment preserves these failures and one interrupted, non-retried attempt; total calls remain capped at 256 and USD 0.50 including a USD 0.01 unknown-billing reserve.

After inspecting raw responses, a separate engineering review accepts only a unique exact echoed output_structure or one redundant trailing closing brace. It never changes expression bytes. Intermediate quantities must execute and match independent rational references. Final expressions must pass the unchanged student grader, or an explicit symbolic event proof that replaces only stated percentage inputs and their complements with symbols and checks the requested independent-event identity. Pure final numbers do not pass this proof. This review is post-output and is not reported as strict-format teacher success.

All raw requests, responses, earlier rejection classifications, proofs, and original results are preserved. The teacher coverage minimum remains 12 accepted questions for every target. Student grading, checkpoint gates, training budgets, and fixed diagnostic questions are unchanged.

Verification: 384 envelope fixtures, 80 symbolic event identities, and 80 answer-only rejection cases passed in the Kaggle environment. No student training had started at the time this review was introduced.
